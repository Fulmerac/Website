Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Secure Life Agency collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. Last updated October 3, 2026.

Information we collect

When you request an interview, you give us your first and last name, email address, the state you live in, whether you are licensed to sell insurance, and your consent to be contacted. Your phone number is optional. When you book a time, the booking page also collects the details it asks for, such as your name, email address and chosen time.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the site running and secure. This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use it

We use your information only to schedule and prepare for your interview, to contact you about this career opportunity, and to keep records of who has applied. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Services we use

Interviews are booked through Google Calendar and held on Google Meet, which are operated by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. If you follow a link to Cole Dillon's Facebook, TikTok or Instagram, those sites' own privacy policies apply.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We share it only with the service providers that help us run this website and schedule interviews, or when the law requires it.

Calls and text messages

If you give us your phone number and agree to be contacted, we may call or text you about your interview and this opportunity. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message or by emailing us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as it is useful for recruiting, and then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, and we will honor them.

Children

This website is intended for adults exploring a career opportunity. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new date.
