An independent life insurance career, paid on commission.

Independent agents help individuals and families choose life insurance that protects the people they care about. You run your own business, with a team behind you.

The work

Contacting prospective clients, understanding their needs, explaining coverage options clearly, and completing applications accurately. The focus is final expense life insurance, along with term life, whole life, burial insurance, and annuities.

How you're paid

By commission on the policies you write. There is no salary. What you earn depends on your activity, skills, and follow-through, and it varies from person to person.

What you need

A state life insurance license before you can sell. You don't need one to apply, and you don't need prior insurance or sales experience. You need to be coachable and comfortable talking with people.

Commission-based work rewards consistency, but it isn't a guaranteed paycheck. Income varies, especially early on. Cole will walk you through how it works in your interview.

Common questions.

How does the commission structure work?

Agents are paid by commission on the policies they write. There is no salary or hourly pay. Commission levels and payment details depend on the carrier and product, and Cole covers them in your interview.

Income varies from person to person and is never guaranteed.

Do I need a life insurance license?

You need a license from your home state before you can sell life insurance, but you don't need one to apply. Getting licensed usually involves a pre-licensing course, a state exam, a background check, and an application to your state's department of insurance. Requirements vary by state.

To help clients in other states, agents add non-resident licenses.

Do I need experience?

No. You don't need a background in insurance or sales. You do need to be coachable, comfortable talking with people, and willing to put in consistent effort.

How independent is the role?

You work as an independent agent and run your own book of business. You choose whether to work part-time or full-time, and you can work from anywhere in the U.S.

Are leads available?

Yes. Agents have access to leads from multiple lead vendors at low, medium, and high levels of intent. Ask Cole how leads are distributed and what they cost.

Which carriers and products?

Agents work with multiple carriers, including Corebridge Financial, Transamerica, Lincoln Financial Group, Mutual of Omaha, and AIG. The focus is final expense life insurance, plus term life, whole life, burial insurance, and annuities.

What happens after I apply?

Your application is reviewed and you'll be contacted by phone or email. You'll book an interview with Cole to talk through the opportunity, and if it's a fit for both of you, you'll discuss licensing and next steps. There's no commitment when you apply.
