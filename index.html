Why join

A career built on people, not pressure.

    You'll learn from Cole directly, not from a binder on a shelf. Ask questions, get feedback, and learn the way the work is actually done.

  2. Work your way

Who we're looking for

The right fit matters more than the right résumé.

Be honest with yourself as you read this. It'll save us both time and make your interview more useful.

You'll likely thrive here if you…

  • Are coachable and act on feedback quickly
  • Stay motivated without someone looking over your shoulder
  • Communicate clearly and genuinely enjoy helping people
  • Are comfortable on video calls and quick to pick up new tools
  • Want to build something long-term, not just fill a gap

It's probably not the right fit if you…

  • Are looking for something passive or hands-off
  • Would rather not be coached or held accountable
  • Can't set aside time to learn and train right now

Are there any costs to get started?

Getting licensed involves a state exam, and agents can buy low-cost leads. We'll go through every cost involved, openly, on your interview call.

How does compensation work?

Agents work as 1099 independent contractors and start at a commission level of at least 100%. We'll go through the specifics on your interview call.

What happens during the interview?

It's a two-way conversation. Cole will get to know your background and goals, walk you through the role, and answer your questions. You don't need to prepare anything beyond being ready to talk.

Do I need insurance or sales experience?

No. Experience isn't required. What matters more is being coachable and willing to put in the work. Training, systems and the team cover the rest.

Do I need to be licensed before I apply?

No. You'll need a life insurance license to work with clients, but you don't need one to interview. If you aren't licensed yet, we'll help you get there.

Is this remote?

It can be. You can work remotely or in person, from anywhere in the 50 states.

Is this full-time or part-time?

Either. You can build this full-time or part-time.

What should I have ready for the call?

A quiet space and a stable internet connection. Google Meet runs in your web browser, or you can use the Meet app on your phone. There's nothing to install on a computer. Bring any questions you have; there are no wrong ones.
